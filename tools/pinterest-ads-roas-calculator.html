What is Pinterest Ads ROAS?

Pinterest Ads ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) measures the amount of revenue your business earns for every dollar spent on Pinterest advertising campaigns. Because Pinterest functions as a visual discovery engine where users actively plan purchases, tracking ROAS helps optimize pin formats, shopping ads, and audience targeting.

Pinterest ROAS = Total Revenue Generated from Pins ÷ Total Pinterest Ad Spend

Why Pinterest Visual Discovery Converts Differently

Unlike traditional interruptive social platforms, users come to Pinterest with high purchase intent during their planning phases. Promoted Pins and Shopping Ads integrate seamlessly into user boards, resulting in higher average order values and long-lasting traffic longevity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROAS for Pinterest Ads?
A ROAS of 4x to 6x (or higher) is generally considered healthy for Pinterest e-commerce campaigns, given the platform's high-intent planning audience.
How long do Pinterest ads continue driving traffic?
Unlike typical social posts that disappear in hours, Pins have an exceptionally long shelf-life and can drive organic and paid discovery for months or even years.
How do I track conversions accurately on Pinterest?
Install the Pinterest Tag on your checkout pages and configure enhanced match parameters along with conversion events.
