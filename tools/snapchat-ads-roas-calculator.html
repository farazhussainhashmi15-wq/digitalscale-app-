What is Snapchat Ads ROAS?

Snapchat Ads ROAS tracks revenue generated relative to your ad spend on Snapchat. Because Snapchat users engage primarily through impulse interactions on mobile, short and punchy vertical creatives drive the best returns.

Snapchat ROAS Formula

Snapchat ROAS = Revenue from Snapchat Ads ÷ Snapchat Ad Spend
