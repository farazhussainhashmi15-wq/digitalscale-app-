What is Snapchat Ads ROAS?

Snapchat Ads ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) measures how much revenue your business generates for every dollar spent on Snapchat advertising. It is the core metric to determine if your vertical video campaigns in regions like Saudi Arabia and the UAE are profitable.

Snapchat ROAS Formula

Snapchat ROAS = Revenue from Snapchat Ads ÷ Snapchat Ad Spend

For example, if you spend $1,000 on Snapchat ads and generate $4,500 in sales, your Snapchat Ads ROAS is 4.5x — meaning every dollar spent returned $4.50 in revenue.

How to Improve Your Snapchat Ads ROAS

1. Use Native Vertical Video Creatives: Ads that look like organic Snapchat stories perform significantly better than polished corporate commercials.

2. Leverage Snap Pixel & Custom Audiences: Retarget users who have previously visited your website or engaged with your profile.

3. Test Localized MENA Influencer Content: Partnering with regional creators for user-generated content (UGC) drives higher trust and lower acquisition costs.

4. Optimize Landing Page Speed: A slow checkout page after a high-converting Snapchat swipe-up will ruin your overall campaign ROAS.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROAS for Snapchat Ads?
A healthy ROAS for Snapchat is typically 4x or higher, depending on your profit margins and product price point.

How is Snapchat Ads ROAS calculated?
Divide the total revenue generated from your Snapchat campaign by the total ad spend allocated to that campaign.

Why is my Snapchat Ads ROAS low?
Low ROAS is usually caused by unengaging creative, poor audience segmentation, or lack of proper pixel tracking setup.
