What is Facebook Ads ROAS?

Facebook Ads ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) measures how much revenue your business earns for every dollar spent on Facebook advertising. It's the key metric for judging whether your Facebook campaigns are actually profitable.

Facebook ROAS Formula

Facebook ROAS = Revenue from Facebook Ads ÷ Facebook Ad Spend

For example, if you spend $1,000 on Facebook ads and generate $4,000 in sales, your Facebook Ads ROAS is 4x — every dollar spent returned $4 in revenue.

How to Improve Your Facebook Ads ROAS

1. Narrow your audience targeting — broad Facebook targeting wastes budget on low-intent viewers.

2. Use the Facebook Pixel and retargeting — retargeting warm audiences on Facebook typically produces 2-3x higher ROAS than cold traffic.

3. Test multiple ad creatives — rotate 3-5 image/video variations and let Facebook's algorithm find the best performer.

4. Improve your landing page speed and clarity — a slow page after a Facebook click kills ROAS even with great ads.

5. Use Lookalike Audiences — built from your existing customers, these often outperform interest-based targeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROAS for Facebook Ads?

A good ROAS for Facebook Ads is typically 4x or higher. E-commerce brands often target 4x-6x, while service businesses may aim for 3x.

How is Facebook Ads ROAS calculated?

Divide the revenue generated from a Facebook ad campaign by the total amount spent on that campaign.

Why is my Facebook Ads ROAS low?

Low ROAS is often caused by broad targeting, weak ad creative, a slow landing page, or not retargeting warm audiences first.
