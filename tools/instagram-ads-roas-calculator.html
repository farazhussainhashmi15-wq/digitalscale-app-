What is Instagram Ads ROAS?

Instagram Ads ROAS measures the income generated per dollar spent on Instagram placements. High-converting Reels and engaging visual storytelling drive strong return rates.

Instagram ROAS Formula

Instagram ROAS = Revenue from Instagram Ads ÷ Instagram Ad Spend
