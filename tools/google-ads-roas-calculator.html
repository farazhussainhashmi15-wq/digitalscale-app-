What is Google Ads ROAS?

Google Ads ROAS measures high-intent search traffic returns. Because users actively search for keywords with strong commercial intent, Google Ads often yields higher baseline performance.

Google Ads ROAS Formula

Google Ads ROAS = Revenue from Google Ads ÷ Google Ad Spend
