Why Track Google Ads ROAS?

Google Ads targets high-intent search traffic, meaning users are actively looking for your product or service. Tracking your Return on Ad Spend ensures your cost-per-click (CPC) translates into sustainable business growth and positive profit margins.

Google Ads ROAS Formula

Google Ads ROAS = Revenue from Google Ads ÷ Google Ads Spend

For example, if you spend $1,500 on Google search ads and generate $6,000 in revenue, your ROAS is 4x — every dollar spent returned $4 in revenue.

How to Improve Your Google Ads ROAS

1. Refine Keyword Match Types: Shift from broad match to phrase and exact match keywords to stop wasting budget on irrelevant clicks.

2. Optimize Landing Page Experience: Ensure your website loads quickly and matches the exact intent of the user's search query.

3. Use Negative Keywords: Regularly review your search term reports to exclude non-converting traffic.

4. Implement Enhanced Conversions: Feed accurate conversion data back to Google's smart bidding algorithms for better optimization.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROAS for Google Ads?
For most search and local lead generation campaigns, a healthy ROAS falls between 3x to 5x.

How is Google Ads ROAS calculated?
Divide the total attributed revenue by the total ad spend on your Google Ads account.

Why is my Google Ads ROAS low?
A low ROAS is often due to poor negative keyword management, low landing page conversion rates, or tracking setup errors.
