What is TikTok Ads ROAS?

TikTok Ads ROAS measures the gross revenue generated relative to your total spend on TikTok advertising. Because TikTok relies heavily on native content style, high creative velocity is key.

TikTok ROAS Formula

TikTok ROAS = Revenue from TikTok Ads ÷ TikTok Ad Spend
